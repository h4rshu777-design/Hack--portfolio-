about

My Journey

I'm a self-learning developer exploring the overlap between coding, automation and artificial intelligence. Most of what I know, I've learned by building — reading docs, breaking things, and fixing them again.

My work leans toward practical tools: Telegram bots, automation scripts, developer utilities, and small experiments that teach me something new about how software — and now AI — actually gets built.

I don't come from a formal background or a big team. This is a one-person, always-learning operation, and Hacks is where that learning becomes something real: shipped code, open projects, and honest updates on what I'm figuring out.

I'm especially curious about how AI is reshaping development — new tools, new workflows, and what it means to build software in this era. I write about that as I go, and I'm always up for collaborating with other developers who are learning the same way.
